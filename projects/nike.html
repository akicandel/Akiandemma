Campaign

Whatever happens

  • Rexona
  • Lola MullenLowe
  • Production: Roma
  • 2024

A campaign about emotional endurance, ritual and loyalty under pressure. Built around the grammar of football but told through intimate, character-first observation, the film moves between anxiety, absurdity, release and belonging.

The visual approach balances cinematic close-ups with a restrained sense of composition, allowing faces, pauses and gestures to carry the weight of the story while keeping the brand present without forcing it.

The campaign extends into a multilingual poster system that preserves the same emotional directness: simple staging, bold type, and the feeling that the promise lands exactly at the moment of greatest tension.
